Upcoming Event | 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (FinTechathon) – International Track

  • Date October 7, 2026
  • Categories Activities, Bachelor, Doctoral, Master, Upcoming Events

📢 Call for Applications

The 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (FinTechathon) – International Track, hosted by Tsinghua University’s PBC School of Finance with WeBank, is now open for applications under the theme “AI as a Financial Participant.”

The competition provides a global platform for students to demonstrate their potential, exchange knowledge in AI-driven financial innovations to address real-world financial scenarios, and explore international career opportunities.

👨🏻‍🎓Eligibility and Teaming

  • Open to full-time enrolled students (Undergraduate, Master’s, and Ph.D. candidates) at overseas universities and research institutions (outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei).
  • Teaming: Teams of 2–5 students (cross-year, cross-major, cross-university, and cross-border collaborations welcomed). Each participant may join only one team across both tracks.
  • Nationalities: To ensure international character, at least 60% of team members must be non-Chinese nationals.

🔥Competition Rules and Topics

  • Official Language: English (for submissions, presentations, and reviews).
  • Topics (Choose One of Six):
    • Topic A: Personal Finance Assistant
    • Topic B: Wealth Advisory Agent
    • Topic C: Cross-border & Student Finance Assistant
    • Topic D: Insurance Assistant
    • Topic E: SME Finance Copilot
    • Topic F: Agent Wallet (Autonomous On-chain Wallet – Includes 0–5 point challenge bonus)

✨Fees and Benefits

  • Registration Fee: No registration fee (100% Free).
  • International Track Prize Pool:RMB 250,000
    • First Prize (1): RMB 100,000
    • Second Prize (1): RMB 80,000
    • Third Prize (1): RMB 50,000
    • Best Innovation Award (1): RMB 20,000
    • Excellence Awards: Several
  • Finalist Benefits: Accommodation and round-trip international travel for invited finalist members arranged and covered by the organizer.
  • Career Fast-Track: Outstanding teams receive fast-track interview access to WeBank.

⏰Timeline

  • Registration & Submission Deadline: 20 October 2026, 23:59 (Beijing Time, UTC+8)
  • Online Review: 20 – 31 October 2026
  • Finalists Announced: 31 October 2026
  • Check-in & WeBank HQ Visit (Shenzhen): 20 November 2026
  • Grand Final & Awards Ceremony (Shenzhen): 21 – 23 November 2026

👉Application Guidelines

Details & Problem Description: Access guidelines via https://drive.scmc.cmu.ac.th/s/2jKErkTJpFmcxYF or the https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/#/en

Registration Form: Download the official form via https://drive.scmc.cmu.ac.th/s/knkpsQd5HpwJ9qj  and attach each member’s passport bio page.

Registration Lodgement: Email the completed registration form and passport bio pages to WANG Yiran (wangyr@pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn) to confirm slot allocation.

Submission: Send the full submission deliverables via email to wangyr@pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn before the deadline.
